Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 1, 2026

SnapScope is a local-only iPhone app for creating field reports. We designed it to work without accounts, backend services, advertising, analytics, or tracking.

Data Collection

SnapScope does not collect personal data from the app. Report projects, notes, statuses, and exported files are stored locally on your device.

Network Requests

The app does not actively send app data to a server. Support and privacy links may open this website in Safari when you choose them.

Photos And Files

If a future version lets you attach photos or files, those items are intended to remain on device unless you choose to export or share them through iOS.

Third Parties

SnapScope does not include third-party advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or tracking SDKs.

隐私政策

最后更新：2026 年 6 月 1 日

现场记是一款用于创建现场报告的本地 iPhone 应用。我们设计它时不使用账号、后端服务、广告、分析或追踪。

数据收集

现场记不会从应用内收集个人数据。报告项目、记录、状态和导出文件都保存在你的设备本地。

网络请求

应用不会主动把应用数据发送到服务器。当你主动点击支持或隐私链接时，系统可能会在 Safari 中打开本网站。

照片和文件

如果未来版本支持附加照片或文件，这些内容也将默认保存在设备本地，除非你主动通过 iOS 导出或分享。

第三方

现场记不包含第三方广告 SDK、分析 SDK 或追踪 SDK。
