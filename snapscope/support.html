Frequently Asked Questions

Does SnapScope require an account?
No. SnapScope works locally on your iPhone.

Where is my data stored?
Your projects and notes are stored on your device. Exported PDF reports are shared only when you choose to share them through iOS.

Does the app work offline?
Yes. The main app workflow does not require an internet connection.

How do I request deletion?
Delete projects inside the app or remove the app from your device. Because SnapScope does not operate an account service, there is no server-side account to delete.

常见问题

现场记需要账号吗？
不需要。现场记在你的 iPhone 本地运行。

我的数据保存在哪里？
你的项目和记录保存在设备本地。导出的 PDF 报告只有在你主动通过 iOS 分享时才会发送出去。

应用可以离线使用吗？
可以。主要工作流程不需要互联网连接。

如何删除数据？
你可以在应用内删除项目，或从设备上删除应用。由于现场记没有账号服务，因此没有服务器端账号需要删除。
