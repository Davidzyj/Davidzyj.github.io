SnapScope

Offline field reports for iPhone.

SnapScope helps you organize site notes, evidence items, severity, and status into a clean local report. It has no account system, no backend, and no tracking.

What it does

  • Create local projects for inspections, maintenance, property checks, and field jobs.
  • Add evidence notes with severity and status.
  • Export a PDF report from your iPhone.
  • Use English or Simplified Chinese.

现场记

适用于 iPhone 的离线现场报告工具。

现场记帮助你把现场记录、证据条目、严重程度和处理状态整理成本地报告。应用无账号、无后端、无追踪。

主要功能

  • 为巡检、维修、物业检查和现场任务创建本地项目。
  • 添加证据记录，并标记严重程度和处理状态。
  • 在 iPhone 上导出 PDF 报告。
  • 支持英文和简体中文。
