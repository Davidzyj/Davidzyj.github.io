Subtitle: Offline field reports from notes

Create clean field reports on your iPhone without accounts, cloud sync, or setup.

SnapScope helps contractors, property managers, cleaners, inspectors, and hands-on teams organize field notes into clear local reports.

Create a project for a site, room, vehicle, or job. Add evidence notes, mark priority, track status, and export a polished PDF when the work needs to be shared. Everything is stored on your iPhone, so the app works even when the job site has no signal.
