CoverCraft Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 29, 2026

CoverCraft is designed as a local iPhone app. The app does not require an account, does not use a backend service, and does not actively upload your photos, videos, covers, or draft information.

Data Collection

CoverCraft does not collect personal data, analytics data, advertising identifiers, or tracking data.

Local Media Processing

When you choose a photo or video from your library, CoverCraft processes that media on your device to create a cover preview and exported image. Imported media is not sent to a server by the app.

Local Storage

Saved drafts are stored locally on your device as lightweight draft information such as title, subtitle, ratio, and template choice. You can delete drafts inside the app.

Third Parties

CoverCraft does not include third-party advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or tracking SDKs.

Support

If you contact support by email, your email address and message will be handled only for support communication.
